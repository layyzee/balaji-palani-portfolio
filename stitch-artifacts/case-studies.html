Case Study 01 Flagship Enterprise Transformation

Scaling FinTech Payments from Monolith to Continuous Delivery

Scope: 6 Squads • 60+ Distributed Software Engineers • Tier-1 Payment Rail Processing

hourglass_bottom 14-Month Engagement hub Scrum of Scrums

Engineering Cadence Floor

Cross-Squad Synchronization Standup • Zurich HQ

Live Release Sync

The Initial State Dilemma

The organization was paralyzed by a monolithic Java architecture. Every deployment required multi-day staging code freezes, manual QA handoffs, and weekend triage rooms. Engineers worked 65+ hours weekly to cope with repetitive sprint carryovers.

Sprint Carryover: 48%
Release Cadence: Monthly Bottleneck
Architectural Shift

“Elena challenged our habit of sizing multi-week epic monsters. She sat with Product Managers to deconstruct value increments into vertical 1-day slices. That single discipline changed everything.”

— Henrik Lindqvist, VP of Engineering

warning

The Challenge

  • Chronic Sprint Spillover: Up to 48% of planned user stories carried over between consecutive iterations.
  • 4-Week Hardened Releases: Releases required complex manual branch merges and midnight sign-off committees.
  • Siloed Functional Segregation: QA was completely disconnected, testing weeks after devs had completed coding.
  • Severe Engineering Burnout: High turnover of senior engineers due to persistent firefighting.
Diagnosis: Friction In Coupling
published_with_changes

The Intervention

  • Scaled Agile Topology: Restructured 6 component silos into feature-focused cross-functional squads containing QA, Dev, and DevOps.
  • Scrum of Scrums Cadence: Instituted 15-minute cross-squad alignment twice weekly to identify upstream architectural friction early.
  • Flow Metrics Governance: Implemented real-time Kanban WIP limits, cycle time heatmaps, and Little's Law queues.
  • Vertical Story Slicing: Ran intensive workshops with POs and tech leads to slice user stories to deliver value in ≤3 days.
Execution: Culture + Flow
